GlobalClip

GlobalClip is a personal tool that finds the best moments in live streams, turns them into short vertical videos with subtitles, and posts them to the owner's own TikTok (@globalclip52) and YouTube accounts after the owner approves each video.

How it works

  1. The tool watches selected Twitch and Kick streams and detects highlight moments.
  2. It cuts a short vertical video with subtitles and sends it to the owner for review.
  3. Only after the owner approves it, the video is posted to the owner's TikTok account using TikTok's Content Posting API.
