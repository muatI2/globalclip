Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026

This policy explains what information GlobalClip ("the app") handles when it connects to TikTok and YouTube, and how that information is used.

Who uses the app

The app is operated by the owner of the TikTok account @globalclip52 for that account only. It is not offered to the public, and no one else can sign in to it.

Information the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic account information (display name and open ID) and permission to publish videos, granted through TikTok Login Kit.
  • YouTube: permission to upload videos to the owner's channel, granted through Google OAuth.
  • Access tokens: the tokens returned by TikTok and Google after the owner signs in.

How the information is used

Tokens are used only to upload videos that the owner has reviewed and approved. The app does not read other users' data, messages, followers, or analytics, and it does not post anything without the owner's approval.

Storage and security

Tokens are stored locally on the owner's own computer and are never sent to any server other than TikTok's and Google's official APIs. The app has no database, website accounts, or analytics trackers.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, or share any information with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) or in the Google Account permissions page, after which the local tokens are deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the TikTok account @globalclip52.
